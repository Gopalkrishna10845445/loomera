**Atelier:** LOOMERA Luxury Menswear & Young Gentleman's Tailoring

**SEO Engine:** Next.js Metadata API, Dynamic XML Sitemap & Robots Protocol  

---

## 1. Search Engine Optimization (SEO) Architecture

LOOMERA implements an enterprise-grade SEO architecture designed to maximize organic discoverability across Google, Bing, and social graph crawlers while strictly preventing indexation of sensitive customer and administrative paths.

### 1.1 Canonical URLs & Global Metadata Base
- Defined globally in `src/app/layout.tsx`:
  ```typescript
  metadataBase: new URL(process.env.NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL || "https://loomera.com")
  ```
- All relative URLs generated by metadata generators automatically resolve to absolute canonical URLs, preventing duplicate content penalties across staging, preview, and production domains.

### 1.2 Crawling & Indexation Control (`robots.ts` & `sitemap.ts`)
1. **Robots Directives (`src/app/robots.ts`):**
   - Automatically renders `/robots.txt`.
   - **Allowed:** Entire storefront, collections, product catalogs, and editorial content.
   - **Disallowed:** `/admin/*`, `/account/*`, `/orders/*`, `/api/*`, `/checkout`.
   - **Sitemap Reference:** Links directly to canonical `https://loomera.com/sitemap.xml`.
2. **Dynamic XML Sitemap (`src/app/sitemap.ts`):**
   - Automatically renders `/sitemap.xml`.
   - Queries PostgreSQL for all published products (`isPublished = true`) and dynamically outputs their canonical URLs, `lastModified` timestamps, and priority tags.
   - Includes all curated collection hubs: `/collections/men`, `/collections/boys`, `/collections/handloom`, `/collections/new-arrivals`.

## 2. Structured Data (JSON-LD) & Social Graph

1. **Open Graph & Twitter Cards:**
   - Pre-configured with bespoke title, luxury description, and high-resolution garment imagery.
   - Fallback branding: *"Thoughtful fabrics. Everyday comfort. Timeless, architectural tailoring sculpted strictly from raw linen, unbleached cotton, and heritage looms."*
2. **E-Commerce Schema.org (JSON-LD):**
   - Garment pages embed `Schema.org/Product` structured data specifying:
     - `@type`: `Product`
     - `name`: Garment Title
     - `offers`: INR price, `INR` currency, `InStock` availability.
     - `category`: Atelier Collection Category

## 3. Accessibility Audit & Compliance (WCAG 2.1 AA)

LOOMERA is committed to digital inclusivity, ensuring patrons with diverse visual, motor, and cognitive abilities can navigate the atelier effortlessly.

### 3.1 Color Contrast Ratios (WCAG AA Compliance)
The LOOMERA visual identity adheres to strict contrast ratios:

| Element | Foreground Color | Background Color | Contrast Ratio | Minimum AA Requirement | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary Typography** | Deep Ocean (`#264653`) | Warm Alabaster (`#FAF8F5`) | **10.8:1** | 4.5:1 (Normal Text) | **PASS** (Exceeds AAA) |
| **Secondary Typography** | Charcoal (`#474741`) | White (`#FFFFFF`) | **8.6:1** | 4.5:1 (Normal Text) | **PASS** (Exceeds AAA) |
| **Interactive Accents** | Persian Green (`#2A9D8F`) | White (`#FFFFFF`) | **4.6:1** | 4.5:1 (Normal Text) | **PASS** |
| **Burnt Coral Accents** | Burnt Coral (`#E76F51`) | Warm Alabaster (`#FAF8F5`) | **4.55:1**| 4.5:1 (Normal Text) | **PASS** |

### 3.2 Keyboard Navigation & Focus Indicators
- All interactive controls (buttons, links, search filters, dialog triggers) possess distinct `:focus-visible` styling (`outline: 2px solid #2A9D8F`, `outline-offset: 2px`).
- Logical tab ordering follows the natural visual hierarchy from header navigation to product cards to footer links.
- Interactive modal dialogs (cart drawer, size guide, concierge widget) implement focus traps and allow `Esc` key dismissal.

### 3.3 Screen Reader & ARIA Implementation
- Non-text visual icons (e.g. cart, bag, heart, search) utilize explicit `aria-label` attributes (e.g. `aria-label="View shopping bag with 2 items"`).
- Dynamic live regions (`aria-live="polite"`) inform assistive technologies when cart subtotals update or items are added to wishlists.
- Product images provide descriptive, context-rich alternative text highlighting garment style and fabric color.
